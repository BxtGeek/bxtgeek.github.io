A hand-built Jekyll theme powering [corpit.org](https://corpit.org). It started
as a migration away from a heavy off-the-shelf theme and grew into a place to
write about storage, Linux, virtualization and DevOps.

- Custom layouts and SCSS (no theme gem)
- Pagefind search, giscus comments, dark mode
- Projects, authors, tags/categories and a yearly archive
