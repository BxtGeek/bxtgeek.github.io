- Raspberry Pi went public on the London Stock Exchange with the ticker symbol RPI .
    - The IPO was successful and the company is now selling shares.

- Tuxedo Computers revealed a new prototype laptop at Computex 2024.
    - This laptop is powered by Qualcomm's Snapdragon X Elite 12 core ARM processor .
    
    - The laptop is expected to be available by Christmas 2024 .

- A new version of openSUSE Leap is now available, Leap 15.6 .
    - This version includes updates to KDE Plasma, GNOME and Xfce .
    
    - Leap 15.6 also includes Cockpit, a web-based graphical interface for servers.

- Mozilla announced the latest version of their web browser, [Firefox 127.0](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/127.0/releasenotes/) .
    - This version includes a lot of great improvements with features and security.

- NetXMS 5.0 is the latest version of this network monitoring tool.
    - This version adds new line styling options for Network maps, styling scripts, automatic bandwidth calculation and more.

- Valve is being sued for allegedly overcharging Gamers for games on Steam even though it's much cheaper than buying on Console.
