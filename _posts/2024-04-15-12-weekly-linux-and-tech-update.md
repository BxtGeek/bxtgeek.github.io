- Fedora is planning to go all in on AI, with a focus on ethical AI.

- Proton has acquired Standard Notes to potentially add a Proton Notes tool to their suite of services.

- Fedora's strategy for 2028 includes reorganizing work groups and focusing on AI as an accelerator and tool.

- Fedora's AI work will be done in collaboration with the open-source world to ensure it aligns with their values and policies.

- Ubuntu 22.04 LTS got its Beta release delayed due to issues with the XZ Library.

- Ubuntu 22.04 will include Gnome 46, power efficiency improvements, and a better gaming experience.

- Explicit sync is coming to the Wayland protocols, which will improve performance and fix glitches and flickering issues for Nvidia users.

- Proton, the makers of Proton Mail and Proton VPN, acquired Standard Notes, an open-source note-taking application.

- KDE is streamlining the way of setting key bindings and keyboard shortcuts in Plasma 6.1.

- Gnome is working on using Nautilus as a provider for the file Chooser portal and implementing encryption of the home directory through Systemd Home.

- League of Legends is losing access to Linux due to Riot Games' implementation of their Vanguard anti-cheat tools.

- Discord removed servers created by new Nintendo Switch emulators like Yuzu and Ryujinx.
