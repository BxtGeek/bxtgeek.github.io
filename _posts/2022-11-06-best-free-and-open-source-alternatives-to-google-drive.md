## What is google drive?

[Google Drive](https://www.google.com/intl/en_in/drive/) is a cloud-based file storage solution. They come with all the google accounts you have. By default, they give lots of features like:

- 15GB of free storage

- Accessibility on any device

- Seamless integration with the other google product.

- Offers tools for sharing and collaboration

## Why consider a google drive alternative?

Many people have a concern about choosing a google drive alternative. Here are a few common ones:

### 1.) Price

By default, google offers 15 Gb of free storage but that is not enough for most people. For that google also offers there paid plans and they are as follows:

100 GB for $1.99/month or $19.99/year  
200 GB for $2.99/month or $29.99/year.  
2 TB for $9.99/month or $99.99/year

### 2.) Compatibility

Google Drive offers great compatibility with google products. But with the other product, the compatibility is not that good.

### 3.) Security

As Google is a big giant and has there monopoly in this space. Due to this amount of centralization, there is a security risk is also associated with this.
