**System76 and the Cosmic Desktop**

- **Reason for Creation**: System76 developed the Cosmic desktop to avoid relying on extensions, which they found to be an unstable foundation for building a unique user experience.

- **Technological Approach**: Instead of forking GNOME, they chose to develop their own desktop environment using the Rust programming language.

- **Customization Features**: Cosmic will include a toggle to theme GTK apps (though not KDE apps for now) and allow users to customize colors and button shapes.

- **Update Strategy**: System76 plans to roll out new features for Cosmic annually, similar to how Windows handles updates.

**Linux Market Share**

- **Global Desktop Market**: Linux has achieved a 4.45% share of the global desktop market, marking a 33% increase over the past year, according to StatCounter.

- **Gaming Market**: On Steam, Linux's market share remains steady at 2.08%.

**Security Study**

- **Vulnerability Findings**: A recent study revealed that 40% of environments are susceptible to complete takeover through privilege escalation attacks.

- **Attack Blocking**: The study found that Linux blocked 65% of these attacks, Windows blocked 62%, and macOS blocked 23%.

- **macOS Security**: The study indicates that macOS is inherently less vulnerable in its default state, but many companies do not invest in further hardening it.

**Distribution Releases**

- **Vanilla OS 2**: This new release supports Android apps and includes graphical tools.

- **Linux Mint Debian Edition Version 6**: This release is based on the latest stable version of Debian.

**CrowdStrike Fallout**

- **Lawsuits**: Delta Airlines is suing CrowdStrike and Microsoft due to an outage that resulted in losses estimated between $350 million and $500 million. Additionally, CrowdStrike is facing a lawsuit from its shareholders following a 32% drop in share price.
