The 2025 vExpert Program is now open for applications! If you're a VMware Enthusiast, this is your chance to join a prestigious group that recognizes your contributions to VMware communities. Here's how you can apply:

### Steps to Apply

1. **Submit Your Application:** Complete the vExpert application form at the [vExpert Portal](https://vexpert.vmware.com/).

3. **Highlight Role:** Clearly mention in which way you can contribute to the this program. If you have some existing blogs or video please add those also that will help you a lot.

5. **Provide Evidence of Contributions:** Include examples such as links to discussions you’ve moderated or screenshots of your Moderator badge.

7. **Attach Supporting Information:** If possible, add details showcasing your community impact and efforts.

### What Happens Next?

Your application will be reviewed by the vExpert Program Manager along with the VMTN team. As a moderator, your chances of acceptance are strong if you've been active and meet the program's basic criteria.

### Why Apply?

- Recognition as a leader in the VMware community

- Access to exclusive resources and events

- Opportunities to collaborate with global VMware experts

If you need help filling out the application form, feel free to reach out to me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bxtgeek/) or [Twitter](https://x.com/bxtgeek). I'll do my best to guide you through the process!

**Don’t wait—apply now and take your contributions to the next level!**
