[Airtel](https://www.airtel.in/thanks/) is now offering a **free 1-year Perplexity Pro subscription** (worth ₹17,000) to all its mobile, broadband, and DTH customers in India. If you're looking for the best way to claim the Airtel Perplexity offer, this guide has you covered from start to finish.

## Step-by-Step: Claim Your Free Perplexity Pro

1. **Open the Airtel Thanks App**
    - Ensure your Airtel number is active and you’re signed into the Airtel Thanks app.

3. **Navigate to Rewards Section**
    - Tap the hamburger menu (☰) at the top-right corner.
    
    - Go to **Rewards and OTTs** or look for “Claim OTTs and more.”

5. **Find the Perplexity Pro Offer**
    - In “Rewards,” locate the **Perplexity Pro** card.

7. **Claim the Subscription**
    - Tap **Claim Now** and then **Proceed**.
    
    - Follow the prompts to sign in with your Google/Apple ID or set up a Perplexity account.
    
    - Once done, your Pro access will be live for 12 months.

## Who Is Eligible?

- All **Airtel mobile**, **broadband**, and **DTH** customers.

- The offer is available from **July 17, 2025, until January 17, 2026**.

- Each user can redeem once. You’ll need to stay an active Airtel customer during the 12-month period.

## What Do You Get with Perplexity Pro?

- **Up to 300 [AI-powered](https://www.corpit.org/category/artificial-intelligence/) searches daily**

- Access to advanced models like **GPT-4.1**, **Claude**, and more

- File upload & analysis (PDFs, spreadsheets, images, etc.)

- **Image generation** and deep research capabilities

- Access to **Labs** for building spreadsheets, apps, dashboards & more

- No credit card needed; the plan won’t auto-renew.
