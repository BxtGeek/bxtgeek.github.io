## What is Project Volterra?

Project Volterra is the Microsoft's Arm kit that comes with the Snapdragon SoC. It also built-in neural processing unit (NPU) built by Qualcomm to perform the powerful AI and ML task. The project looks so promising. As per the rumours the product code name is Black Rock and it's built by the Surface team in partnership with Qualcomm.

As of now there are not lots of specs and feature is available in the public. Due to current chip shortage it will take time to come to general public.

First batch will definitely come to United state and later on it will come to other counties.
